INTEL HACKATHON CONCEPT

SAME ROOTS.
DIFFERENT MISSIONS.

Two characters. One shared purpose. Different ways to turn challenges into solutions.

What connects them?

Both characters share these core characteristics:

Problem-solving Curiosity Adaptability Innovation
N

NOVA

THE STRATEGIST

Nova studies information, identifies patterns, predicts problems and creates the best plan.

Mission: Think and plan
1. Collects information
2. Analyzes possible solutions
3. Predicts risks
4. Recommends the best action
B

BOLT

THE BUILDER

Bolt uses tools, transforms plans into action, builds solutions and tests the results.

Mission: Build and act
1. Receives a plan
2. Chooses the right tools
3. Builds the solution
4. Tests and improves the result

Different Roles. One Goal.

Nova figures out what to do.
Bolt focuses on how to do it.

Together, they show how different abilities can work toward the same goal.
